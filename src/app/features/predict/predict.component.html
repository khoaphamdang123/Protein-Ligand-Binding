How to read the results

  • Each ligand class has its own model and threshold, so probabilities are not comparable across classes. Metal uses a high threshold: few residues are marked, but most of them are correct.
  • Nucleic acid answers "does it bind DNA or RNA?"; DNA and RNA are separate models that answer for one of them. They are different questions: do not combine their results.
  • Where pLDDT is below {{ lowPlddt }}, the predicted structure, and the structure-based prediction there, is less reliable.
